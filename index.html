As a Fall 2026 UROC Koret Scholar, I collaborate with research mentor Dr. Lev Horodyskyj (Science Voices / Blue Marble Space Institute of Science) and my lab partner Daniel Orta to engineer Orbital Sentinel—a mission control dashboard that streams live flight telemetry and tracks near-Earth satellite orbits in 3D.

Coming from the agricultural heartland of South Monterey County, I have made it my mission to ensure that advanced systems engineering and space science are not walled off by economic privilege. I direct community maker spaces, deploy open hardware build guides, and conduct field workshops that empower the next generation of rural engineers.

Research Interests & Framework

Core Research Hook

"Access to space science should not be limited by zip codes, institutional prestige, or multimillion-dollar budgets. My research democratizes aerospace engineering and remote sensing for rural, underrepresented communities. By pairing everyday microcontrollers like the ESP32 with accessible web-based flight dashboards, we transform consumer drones and high-altitude balloons into practical training analogs for orbital satellites. Through this hands-on framework, students build physical environmental sensors, stream live telemetry, and analyze real-world planetary data—bridging the gap between fundamental classroom concepts and real flight operations to cultivate the next generation of engineers."

Full-Stack Telemetry Pipelines

Designing high-throughput ground station web architectures using Node.js, Express, MySQL, and dynamic client-side AJAX polling. We ingest real-time sensor streams without browser latency and model orbital dynamics using 3D CelesTrak satellite ephemeris data.

Terrestrial Flight Analogs

Decoupling space operations pedagogy from costly orbital launches. Utilizing Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) and High-Altitude Sensing Balloons (HASB) as empirical testing grounds for sensor payloads, RF communications, and NASA Science Traceability Matrices (STMs).

Needs-Responsive STEM Equity

Addressing geographic and socio-economic education barriers in the Salinas Valley. Designing bilingual, modular hardware build guides (Greenworks Workshop) and mobile labs that build verified confidence in circuit design and embedded software programming.
